# Glossary

The terms, collected in one place. Each
is defined where it first appears, too;
this page is for the second encounter.

- **Account** — a place money sits or
  flows through: a bank account, a card,
  an expense category, an income source.
- **Accounting equation** — Assets =
  Liabilities + Equity + (Income −
  Expenses). The identity every balanced
  book satisfies (chapter 1).
- **Asset** — what you have: balances,
  savings, things of value.
- **Balance sheet** — a report of one
  date: assets against liabilities plus
  equity (chapter 9).
- **Book** — one self-contained set of
  accounts, stored in one file.
- **Commodity** — a kind of thing an
  account holds. Currencies are
  commodities; so are shares.
- **Credit** — the accounting word for
  the right-hand side of an entry: money
  arriving in a liability, equity or
  income account, or leaving an asset.
  One half of the debit/credit pair.
- **Debit** — the left-hand side: money
  arriving in an asset or expense
  account, or leaving a liability,
  equity or income account.
- **Double-entry** — recording every
  movement twice, as a from and a to, so
  the legs always cancel.
- **Engine** — the part of GnuCash that
  checks transactions and computes
  balances, whoever is driving it.
- **Equity** — what was yours before the
  period began: opening balances and
  past gains, in the book's terms.
- **Imbalance account** — the alarm
  account GnuCash books an uncancelling
  remainder to. Zero in a healthy book.
- **Income statement** — a report over a
  period: income less expenses. Also
  called Profit & Loss.
- **Liability** — what you owe: a card
  balance, a loan.
- **Placeholder account** — a grouping
  account that holds no money itself;
  its balance is its children's total.
- **Reconcile** — to agree a book account
  with an outside statement, entry by
  entry, until the difference is zero.
- **Register** — the ledger page of one
  account in the window, where its
  transactions are entered and shown.
- **Split** — one leg of a transaction:
  an account and a signed amount.
- **Transaction** — one movement of
  money, made of splits that sum to
  zero.
- **Transfer account** — in a simple
  entry, the other half: the account the
  money came from or went to.
- **Trial balance** — the sum of every
  split in the book; zero when the books
  are internally complete.
